## Technical evidence and boundaries

Lemo: Node 20+, FFmpeg, Python 3.11+ or uv documented; Playwright browser, Canvas 2D, three.js/WebGL2 and optional sound/voice packages. Contract window.READY, window.DUR, window.render(t); deterministic seeded frame evaluation; common timeline drives generated picture/music/SFX/subtitles. It supports --size 1080x1920, though individual examples contain hardcoded 1920x1080 geometry and need vertical composition changes. DIRECTOR and TECHNIQUE are aimed largely at original narrated scored films. For this user's Suno master, take the visual/timing methods and derive cues from the existing recording. Keep all music replacement, foley, TTS, mandatory silences, mix ducking, fades and loudnorm processing as explicitly chosen changes to a separate output/version.

Actual core/render/video.mjs streams JPEG screenshots into one FFmpeg MP4 segment per worker, joins segments and atomically renames final output. It locks output files and cleans abandoned segment directories; its cleanup deletes segments on normal completion, failure or interruption. There is no retained-frame resume mechanism in the inspected renderer. Deterministic seeking enables rerendering a timestamp, which is distinct from recovery of already rendered frames. Preserve the kit's tested local recovery path or adapt an explicitly versioned durable frame store if adopting Lemo techniques.

Opus painted: p5.js+p5.brush, Puppeteer Chrome and FFmpeg; kinetic: Canvas 2D+three.js+WebGL post. Frames are pure functions of t. Each shot paints its complete frame. Cues live separately from artwork. Painted chapter files use private IIFEs with shared cast/palette helpers. Kinetic cue file records scene starts S, hits, CH chapters and duration; SCENES/TR registers drawing functions/transitions. Artist-specific palettes, landscapes, signature mascots and synth scores are examples, not project defaults.

Both opus templates have resumable --frames mode: inspect for missing frames or size < 1000 bytes; write .tmp then rename JPEG; --range permits bounded regeneration; --encode separate. The cache test is existence/size, not identity of source+audio+render settings. Versioned output folders and invalidation of changed scene/frame ranges are necessary to avoid stale frames. Hardware speed quotes in SKILL.md are anecdotal for its author's environment, not measured in this kit.

Important music precision: beat_grid.py computes spectral-flux onsets, autocorrelation search 70–170 BPM, one global beat phase and supplied LRC-line beat indices. It does not establish variable tempo, a true first bar/downbeat, individual word timing or every expressive vocal pickup. Verify pulse/phase against several points in the exact recording and use section maps for tempo changes. Actual karaoke.js computes a continuous left-to-right clipping fraction across a whole line from opt.sing or a default duration. Its README/SKILL 'word-by-word' wording should be presented as a visual fill effect, not demonstrated word-level forced alignment. Supply validated word intervals when exact syllable/word highlighting matters.

Use the following style families as a vocabulary for interpreting our selected song. Painterly journal: build translucent strokes over warm paper, reveal the structure before detail, let a recurring accent identify the lyric's central object, and use brush growth, mist erasure or a travelling painted world as transitions. Seed generated strokes and cache completed paintings; animate the few marks that are changing. A portrait frame can travel vertically through a tree, city, memory or growing system. Choose this when the lyrics benefit from intimacy, observation, remembrance or transformation.

Risograph and printed layers: choose two or three translucent spot inks with useful overprint mixtures, paper-colored knockouts, stable registration offsets and large silhouettes. Let an additional ink enter at a musical or narrative turn. A brief registration kick can emphasize a hit; restrained drift can express uncertainty. Keep the camera smooth while selected character drawings update at a deliberate limited-animation cadence. For opaque silkscreen treatment, stack flat ink layers, stepped color bands and squeegee reveals; re-ink recurring geometry as the story changes time or mood. Specify which printing action carries the song's meaning and show how the chorus advances it.

Grid and kinetic typography: assign a readable hierarchy to lead lyric fragments, supporting images and short annotations. Choose one signal color, a grid adapted to 1080×1920, and modular graphic movements. Arrivals can land on measured musical cues with clip reveals and precise easing. A deliberate exception to an established visual rule can embody a lyric's rebellion, loss of control or breakthrough. In the kinetic family, combine selected letter staggers, masked shader textures, counters, shapes and depth layers with an actual scene mechanism: a connection forms, an object changes state, a network opens or a shape condenses. Use numbers according to their meaning in the song; label interpretive diagrams where needed. Make essential words readable at phone size and use microtype as secondary texture.

Cel animation and character acting: separate painted background, flat shaded character, luminous elements and restrained playback texture. Create a model sheet with several views, expressions and key poses before building the proof. Keep the character's silhouette and focal expression clear against its background. Use anticipation, the principal action and follow-through; blend poses smoothly; place held objects consistently in the hands. Limited drawings can update at 12 or 8 fps while rain, light and camera move at output frame rate. A held close-up with changing light can carry a sustained vocal; a brief impact drawing can punctuate a musical peak. Check scanline and grain detail after downscaling to a phone preview.

Continuous line and shadow theatre: a line can become a road, body, word or remembered object, carrying one visual idea through successive lyric moments. Plan connection topology and test the final whole drawing early so each reveal reads at its final scale. For shadow puppets, use translucent cut pieces, visible joints, glowing carved openings and overlapping layers. Character attitude comes from profile, head angle, body tilt and deliberate staging. Precompute any pendulum motion to support reproducible seeking. ## Incorporation-ready guidance

### Select an animation method that serves the song

Treat the music-video skill as a hybrid production reference. It combines generated identity sheets and animated character performances from paid Fal services with local p5 graphics, Python analysis and cutouts, optional Swift effects, and FFmpeg assembly. Its code and documentation use MIT licensing. Model services, selected media, fonts and external examples carry their own usage conditions. Build the first proof from the current local renderer and a small number of purposeful assets; propose a generated performance only when a specific lyric or emotional action needs it. Name that shot, its required asset, estimated calls, and its local alternative. Keep the chosen Suno recording as the canonical soundtrack throughout.

Choose one coherent material language from the song. Describe its linework, surface, shading, lighting, colour relationships, typography and camera behaviour in observable terms. A hand-painted approach can use flat washes, hatching, ink outlines, paper texture and gently changing drawings. An illustrated collage can combine stable background plates, isolated subjects and local cameras. A graphic approach can use drawn shapes, measured typography and visual metaphors. Express the meaning through a focal action: a subject seeks, discovers, changes, resists or resolves something. Give the opening and strongest musical or narrative payoff early representation in the proof plan.

### Build callbacks and legible acting

Develop a recurring object through distinct story functions: introduce it, let a verse change its significance, transform it at a musical peak, and resolve it near the ending. Specify the viewer's primary focal subject and reading order in every shot. Give important actions anticipation, a clear peak pose, follow-through and a readable settle. Use offset timing for secondary parts, characters and crowds so their relationships feel intentional. Connect emotion changes through expression and body action. Select hold lengths by what the phone viewer needs to recognise; musical speed and visual density can vary independently. Let the central action complete, then land the next transformation.

ClaudeAnimationBase offers useful pure-time motion helpers: damped springs for secondary settling; eased keyframes; arcs for throws or jumps; prepared squash and stretch; camera moves; and optional twelve-drawings-per-second holds. Its boilSeed(key) reseeds each element using a stable identity and held drawing index, keeping the appearance of stationary objects consistent while other objects move. Translate these mechanisms into the chosen aesthetic. Typography belongs in the plan whenever the song benefits from sung words, a title, labels or an expressive lyric moment. Adopt the base's staging and timing principles while specifying the text treatment required by this project.

### Separate musical evidence from estimates

Create analysis copies of the canonical recording and keep their roles visible. Analyse onsets, loudness and beat candidates, listen to the whole selected version, and document confirmed phrases, vocal entrances, pauses, instrumental changes and ending. The makevoid beat script fits a steady tempo primarily within 80–180 BPM and assigns four-beat bar positions using low-frequency energy. Treat its grid as an initial hypothesis; confirm meter, first downbeat, half/double tempo and drift against the actual song. Maintain corrected timing as song-time seconds and frame ranges. Sung-word transcription and separated vocals can help difficult passages, with audible review confirming the words and alignment.

Use inclusive start and exclusive end frames. Each section's ending frame equals the next section's starting frame; local scene time equals song time minus the section start. Supply each worker the same source-master identity, frame rate and boundaries. Choose the proof around completed lyric phrases and musical events, targeting the established 15–25-second scope with a specific justified boundary. For a singing face, plan a close or medium composition, visible mouth and a matching vocal segment; judge the performance with sound. For a graphic-only scene, synchronise the selected meaningful cues and let background motion support them.

### Make source production recoverable

Separate accepted identity references, scene assets, timing, source code, derived frames and exports. Assign parallel workers distinct scene resources and output paths; let one coordinator own shared manifests and final assembly. Each job records dependencies, exact frame interval, reference version, deliverables, review evidence, budget and retry allowance. Preserve provider request IDs and receipts so a paused generation can be resumed deliberately. Accept the result after inspection of its identity, staging and timed action, then unblock dependent jobs. Rebuild the downstream material affected by a changed reference while retaining previous accepted versions.

Design graphics as deterministic functions of absolute or explicitly derived local time, using stable seeds for procedural variation. ClaudeAnimationBase's saved-frame path writes JPEGs atomically and schedules missing frames, supporting interruption recovery; its direct clip path streams frames to FFmpeg. The makevoid p5 renderer produces named PNGs and offers selected frame indices, while its job and model-request manifests preserve generation state. Select the recovery mechanism actually provided by the chosen renderer and record a reproducible restart action in the checkpoint. Use a versioned render directory whenever source or timing changes.

### Adapt the workflow to the vertical proof

Both upstream projects favour landscape defaults; specify 1080×1920 composition throughout this kit's brief and renderer configuration. Stage the subject, lyric blocks, camera movement, cutouts and reading order inside that canvas. Review representative first, middle and last shot frames, adjacent-frame strips for key motion, detail crops for expressions and cutout edges, and the proof in playback with the canonical audio. A contact sheet establishes composition and coverage; timed playback establishes pacing, transitions and vocal alignment.

Select a small set of fonts with usable licenses and the required glyphs; record family, weight, source and bundled project filename where redistribution permits. Wait for font and image readiness before capture. The makevoid toolkit prepares selected installed font files per project; portability additionally requires recording their usage rights and fallback. Benchmark representative scenes locally before extending to a full song. Brush watercolour fills can be expensive, so use measured frame times to choose texture complexity and worker counts. Treat upstream cost or runtime examples as observations from those productions; estimate this project's tokens, asset calls, storage and render time from its own proof.
