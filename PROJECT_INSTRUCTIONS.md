## Production sequence

1. Read the pasted Grok brief, approved lyrics and attached master. Measure the recording's full duration and map its complete structure. Plan the full-song visual arc by default; use a shortened excerpt only when the user explicitly asks.
2. Before still output, use the no-media plan and the risk-based sample procedure in `claude-finished-song-animation-project/07_QUALITY_REVIEW_AND_ACCEPTANCE.txt`. Select the smallest set that covers the opening/end, distinct scene states, important lyric/type treatments, risky seams and identified fixes; do not sample uniformly by default. After each authorized full candidate, use the temporal scan in guide 18 for frame flicker/dropouts before word-level lyric polish.
3. Implement and render the complete measured-duration candidate only after its applicable authorization. After complete-candidate playback is authorized, review the full video at normal speed with sound once; replay only passages where a question remains.
4. Make bounded, evidence-based corrections. Re-render affected stills for local visual fixes and render a new complete candidate only when required to review changed motion or the integrated result. Record technical, alignment, visual and user-acceptance evidence separately.

Before coding, follow guide 19 and copy both storyboard templates into the song's storyboard folder. Fill one CSV row for every second of the measured duration, linking each to cue/event, scene, visible state/action and a board panel or reasoned hold. Draw per-scene SVG panels for entry, development, payoff and each distinct setup/action change; do not force a cut every second. Link panels to component callers. Keep the board separate from risk-based still and playback evidence.

The first playable candidate is the complete full-song video at the recording's measured duration. Set explicit `PROJECT.duration`; let an omitted maximum follow it. For a zero-offset full master, the export plan rounds video frame count up, keeps Remotion's scheduled source-audio span at the exact measured duration, and reports the planned endpoint difference (under one frame). This is a source-plan fact, not proof of encoded endpoints or synchronization; verify those only after authorized render and playback. Do not trim, pad or otherwise alter the supplied master without a separate decision. Use source inspection and an authorized risk-based still set for intermediate visual checks.
